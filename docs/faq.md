# Noryx FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the Noryx Platform.

## What is Noryx Finance?

Noryx Finance develops quantitative technology for digital markets.

The Noryx Platform combines market intelligence, quantitative analysis, risk controls, analytics and exchange connectivity within a systematic trading environment.

## Is Noryx a trading bot?

Noryx is presented as a quantitative trading platform rather than a simple trading bot.

The platform includes multiple technology layers such as:

- Noryx Market Intelligence
- Noryx Quant Engine
- Noryx Risk Engine
- Noryx Execution Layer
- Noryx Analytics
- Noryx Connect

## Does Noryx guarantee profits?

No.

Noryx does not guarantee profits, returns or future trading performance.

Digital asset trading involves substantial risk.

## Is Noryx financial advice?

No.

Noryx provides software, quantitative market technology and educational research.

Nothing provided through the platform constitutes financial, investment or trading advice.

## Does Noryx hold user funds?

Noryx is designed to interact with supported external exchanges through API-based connectivity.

Users maintain their exchange accounts and assets with the external exchange.

## Should withdrawal permissions be enabled?

Withdrawal permissions should remain disabled whenever they are not required for platform functionality.

Users should apply the minimum API permissions necessary.

## What is the Noryx Quant Engine?

The Noryx Quant Engine is the core quantitative processing layer of the platform.

It evaluates multiple market variables to assess systematic trading conditions.

## What is Signal Confidence?

Signal Confidence is a composite analytical measure used to evaluate the quality of a potential trading condition.

It is not a guarantee of success.

## What is Market Regime?

Market regime refers to the broader market environment.

Examples may include:

- trending
- ranging
- expansion
- compression
- high volatility
- low volatility

Noryx can use market regime as contextual information when evaluating signals.

## Can Noryx decide not to trade?

Yes.

A core principle of systematic risk management is the ability to reject opportunities when required conditions are not satisfied.

## What is the Noryx Risk Engine?

The Risk Engine evaluates whether a potential trading opportunity satisfies predefined risk conditions before execution.

## What is Noryx Analytics?

Noryx Analytics provides visibility into system and strategy behavior.

Depending on available data, this may include:

- signal history
- trade history
- P&L
- drawdown
- confidence analytics
- risk events
- market-regime analysis

## What is the difference between Live, Demo and Backtest?

### Live

Real trading activity through connected exchange infrastructure.

### Demo

Simulated trading without real capital exposure.

### Backtest

Historical simulation using past market data.

These environments should not be interpreted as equivalent.

## Are backtest results reliable?

Backtests can help evaluate historical strategy behavior, but they are hypothetical.

They may not fully account for:

- slippage
- liquidity
- latency
- fees
- exchange outages
- changing market conditions

Backtested results are not indicative of future performance.

## Which exchanges are supported?

Only exchanges explicitly shown as supported within the Noryx Platform should be considered compatible.

Supported integrations may change over time.

## How are exchange accounts connected?

Supported exchange accounts can be connected using API credentials generated directly within the user's exchange account.

## Is my API key safe?

Exchange credentials are sensitive information and should only be entered through official Noryx interfaces.

Public documentation does not disclose Noryx's internal credential-management implementation.

Users should also follow exchange security best practices.
