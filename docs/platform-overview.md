# Noryx Platform Overview

Noryx Finance develops quantitative infrastructure for digital markets.

The Noryx Platform combines market intelligence, quantitative analysis, adaptive risk evaluation and exchange connectivity within a unified systematic trading environment.

## Core Architecture

The platform is organized around several public-facing technology layers:

### Noryx Market Intelligence

Interprets market context using signals derived from:

- market structure
- momentum
- volatility
- volume
- multi-timeframe conditions
- directional strength

### Noryx Quant Engine

The core quantitative processing layer used to evaluate systematic trading opportunities.

The engine combines multiple market variables to assess signal quality and directional probability.

### Noryx Risk Engine

Evaluates whether market conditions satisfy predefined risk constraints before execution.

Risk evaluation may consider:

- volatility
- signal confidence
- market regime
- exposure limits
- execution conditions

### Noryx Execution Layer

Connects validated trading decisions with supported exchange infrastructure.

Execution is subject to strategy and risk controls.

### Noryx Connect

Provides the connectivity layer between the Noryx Platform and supported external exchanges.

### Noryx Analytics

Provides system and strategy analytics including:

- signal history
- trade history
- confidence analytics
- performance metrics
- system behavior

## Platform Principles

Noryx is designed around several core principles:

- systematic decision-making
- measurable market conditions
- adaptive risk management
- transparent analytics
- controlled execution
